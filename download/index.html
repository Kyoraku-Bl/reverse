How to run it

  1. Unzip the archive — you get a single kyorakureplay/ folder.
  2. Serve it (this also starts the live-data proxy):
    cd kyorakureplay && python3 server.py
  3. Visit http://localhost:8000 — sign up, then open replay.html. The chart badge reads LIVE · Yahoo Finance or LIVE · Binance; opening the files without server.py makes the badge say SIMULATED.
  4. To publish: drop the folder on Vercel — api/yahoo.js and api/binance.js become the live endpoints automatically.
Real prices from Yahoo Finance & Binance — simulated candles only ever appear with an explicit label. No seeded data: your journal starts at zero. Design study, not affiliated with FX Replay Inc.
