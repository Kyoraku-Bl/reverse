FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the data real?

Yes. Every candle is the venue's own OHLCV. Futures and indices come from CME/Cboe via the Yahoo Finance chart API, crypto from Binance's public REST API. When the paid CME feed is configured (Databento), futures come straight from Globex instead.

Why does NAS100 differ from NQ?

NAS100 is the Nasdaq-100 cash index; NQ is the E-mini future on that index. They track each other but the future carries basis and trades nearly 24 hours.

How much history do I get?

Up to 1,800–3,000 intraday candles per timeframe and around ten years of daily candles, depending on what the provider serves for that symbol. The CSV button exports exactly what the chart holds.

Do I need an account?

You can replay as a guest. An account keeps your watchlist, drawings, positions and journal under your email on this browser.

Is this FX Replay?

No. KyorakuReplay is an independent study inspired by replay tools, not affiliated with FX Replay or TradingView.
