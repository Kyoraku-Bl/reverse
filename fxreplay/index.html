KyorakuReplay is a browser-based backtesting and trading practice platform. It replays real historical price data across Futures, Forex, Stocks, Indices, Metals, Crypto and Energy so you can test strategies and practice execution without risking real money. Market replay, a trading journal, Mentor AI, a prop firm simulator and custom indicators all live in one platform.
